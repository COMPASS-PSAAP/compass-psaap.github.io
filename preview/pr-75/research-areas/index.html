Research Areas

The center organizes its work into seven areas. Each section below describes an area, lists the projects working in it, and links to its publications.

Performance ModelingCross-Layer OptimizationSolver DesignSystem SoftwareIntegrationProject InfrastructureEducation

Performance Modeling

Analytical and empirical models that predict how applications and system software behave at scale, and the measurement infrastructure that grounds them.

SparseAtlas

SparseAtlas is developing a curated and computationally characterized collection of sparse matrices. Its goal is to help researchers understand how sparse-matrix structure influences computational behavior across hardware architectures, algorithms, and scientific workloads.

performance-modeling
Beatnik

Beatnik is being redesigned to model the rising bubble problem. The new Beatnik uses unstructured, adaptive meshing and a fast multipole solver for far field interactions. The library uses Cabana for GPU-optimized array of structures of arrays (AoSoA) data structures and Kokkos to run on GPUs.

Cross-Layer Optimization

Optimizations that span application, runtime, and hardware boundaries, exposing information across layers that are traditionally opaque to one another.

System Software

Runtimes, communication libraries, and operating system services that applications depend on for portable performance.

Stream Triggering Design and Implementation

Design, implementation, and evaluation of a stream-triggered MPI interface, initially prototyped and tested on the LLNL Tuolumne and ORNL Frontier HPE Slingshot Systems.

system-software
Stream Triggering MPI Standardization

Research to refine and fully specify the syntax and semantics of our stream triggering MPI operations and have them added to the MPI standard as a new “Queued Communications.” The link points to a recent draft of the standard that includes this chapter.

system-software integration
Stream Triggered Kokkos Comm

Implementation of stream triggering as a backend in Kokkos Comm. Tested on Tuolumne.
